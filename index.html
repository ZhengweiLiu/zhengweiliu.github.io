About Me

I am a Lecturer at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Nanjing Normal University, where I have been working since July 2021.

I received my Ph.D. in Probability and Mathematical Statistics from Jilin University in June 2021, under the supervision of Professor Fukang Zhu. I received my B.Sc. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Jilin University in June 2016.

Research Interests

My research focuses on time series analysis and Bayesian statistics. I am interested in developing statistical methods for dependent data, with applications to traffic and infectious diseases.

  • Count time series analysis
  • Gaussian processes
  • Dynamic linear models
  • Semiparametric models
  • Panel quantile regression
  • Underreported data analysis
  • Network time series
